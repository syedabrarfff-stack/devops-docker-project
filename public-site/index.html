What she does

Everything your front desk does on the phone.

Not a menu tree, not a callback form. Sarah handles the whole conversation and acts on it — writing to your schedule, sending the confirmation, and escalating what genuinely needs a human.

Answers every call

No hold music, no voicemail, no engaged tone. She picks up on the first ring at 3am as readily as at 3pm, and takes several callers at once.

Books into your schedule

She checks what is genuinely free, offers real times, and writes the appointment straight to your calendar — never double-booking a chair or a dentist.

Reschedules and cancels

Patients move appointments by phone at any hour. The slot is freed instantly and offered to the next caller, instead of quietly going empty.

Confirms by SMS

Every booking, change and cancellation is confirmed in writing to the patient. Reminders go out before the appointment, which is what actually cuts no-shows.

Recognises returning patients

She looks a caller up mid-conversation, so an existing patient is not asked to spell their name and history back to you every single time.

Escalates real emergencies

When a call needs a person, she hands it to your team during hours, or texts your on-call number immediately after them — with the callback number first.

Interrupts naturally

Talk over her and she stops, listens, and picks up where you left off. Pause mid-sentence and she waits rather than answering half a thought.

Arabic and English

She follows whichever language the patient opens with, and mirrors it for the rest of the call — no menu, no "press 2 for English".

Answers the routine questions

Opening hours, location, parking, which insurers you accept, what a treatment involves and roughly what it costs — the calls that eat your desk's day.
