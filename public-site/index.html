Questions

What clinic owners usually ask.

Will it sound robotic?+
No — Sarah has a natural, warm conversation and adjusts to how the caller speaks. Most patients don't realize they aren't speaking with a person at your front desk. The best way to judge is to call her yourself at (947) 600-5912.
What happens if she can't answer a question?+
Sarah knows what she knows — and knows when to hand off. For anything outside her scope, she'll take a message or transfer the call to your team, so nothing falls through the cracks.
Can I keep my existing phone number?+
Yes. Nothing changes for your patients — they call the number they already have.
What if I need a human transferred in?+
Sarah can transfer any call to your front desk or on-call staff at any point in the conversation — she's built to hand off smoothly, not to gatekeep.
