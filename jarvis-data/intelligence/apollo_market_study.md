## Market Position Overview

Apollo.io has established itself as the dominant B2B sales intelligence and engagement platform for SMB and mid-market sales teams. With over 275 million contact records and 60 million company profiles, it serves approximately 160,000 companies globally — predominantly in North America, UK, and Australia.

The platform's sweet spot is the 10-500 employee company with an outbound-focused sales motion. Its core value proposition is data access and email sequence automation, not operational execution. This creates a structural gap that Aliyar Solutions can fill: Apollo finds the leads, but it cannot build the infrastructure to convert them at scale.

Apollo's average customer pays $99–$479/month for data access and basic sequencing. They are not building custom AI workflows, CRM architecture, or operational systems. They are sales teams, not technology teams. This is our opening.
