## 16. Development Conventions

- **Active branch:** `claude/jarvis-cans-api-integration-ZThTD`
- **No `debug=True` in production — ever**
- **All new routes:** register in `backend/app/api/v1/__init__.py`
- **All new views:** add to `frontend/src/App.jsx` VIEWS map + `Sidebar.jsx` NAV

- **All new models:** import in `backend/app/models/__init__.py`
- **Commit format:** `feat(phase-N): short description`
- **Secrets:** `.env` only — gitignored, never committed
